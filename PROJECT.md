# Leverage OS
## Mission
Help people achieve 10 years of progress in 1 year.
## Vision
Become the world's first AI Decision Operating System.
## Core Principle
Leverage AI to multiply human judgment, execution and learning.
## Product Philosophy
- Better decisions > More work
- Assets > Tasks
- Validation > Planning
- Cashflow > Vanity
- Learning > Busyness
- Compounding > Linear Growth
## Success Metric
Every feature must help users:
1. Make better decisions.
2. Execute faster.
3. Learn faster.
4. Build reusable assets.
5. Compound results over time.
If it does none of these, don't build it.
